Where and how we invest

Six focus areas, one approach: patient capital, clear terms, and close support.

Focus areas

  • Sustainable infrastructure

    We back energy, water, and logistics assets that serve communities for decades. We look for stable demand, clear contracts, and operators who plan for maintenance as carefully as for growth.

    In the portfolio Lumora Energy, Tirta Grid

  • Digital and technology

    We invest in software and digital services that make established industries work better. We favour products with paying customers, honest metrics, and teams that ship steadily.

    In the portfolio Corevia Labs

  • Consumer and retail

    We support consumer and retail businesses that earn loyalty through quality and fair sourcing. We pay close attention to supply chains, store economics, and how a brand treats its customers.

    In the portfolio Patdua, Nema, Pasar Hijau, Tandra Home

  • Agriculture and food

    We work with growers, processors, and distributors who make food supply more reliable. Traceability, fair prices for producers, and resilience to weather and market swings guide our choices.

    In the portfolio Kebun Kita, Satya Mills

  • Healthcare

    We invest in clinics, diagnostics, and health services that widen access without cutting corners on quality. We expect clear clinical standards and transparent pricing.

    In the portfolio Harbor Health

  • Financial services

    We back financial tools that help small businesses keep records, manage cash, and borrow responsibly. Sound risk management and clear customer terms matter more to us than fast growth.

    In the portfolio Nusa Ledger

How a partnership works

  1. 1

    Source

    We meet founders and operators through sector networks and referrals.

  2. 2

    Evaluate

    We test the business model, the team, and the data behind the plan.

  3. 3

    Partner

    We agree on terms, governance, and the support we will provide.

  4. 4

    Grow

    We help with hiring, operations, and access to new markets.

  5. 5

    Review

    We review progress against agreed measures and adjust together.

What we look for

The same four questions apply in every sector.

  • Sound economics

    A business model that works at the unit level, not only on a forecast.

  • Capable, candid management

    People who share bad news early and ask for help when they need it.

  • Responsible practices

    Fair treatment of staff, suppliers, customers, and the environment.

  • Room to grow

    A credible path to growing steadily over many years.
